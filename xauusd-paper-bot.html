Strategy rules (what the bot does)

Bias — flip on Break of Structure / Change of Character (close through last confirmed swing).

Setup — longs only in discount (< range EQ), shorts only in premium (> EQ). Entry when price retests an unmitigated FVG or Order Block within 24 candles of the structure shift.

Confirmation — entry candle must close in the trade direction and have positive candle delta (longs) / negative (shorts). Delta is a tick-volume proxy: vol × (2·(close−low)/(high−low) − 1).

Sessions — optional filter: London 07:00–10:00 UTC, NY 12:00–15:00 UTC (candle open time).

Risk — 1% of current balance per trade; SL beyond protected swing/OB (buffer = max(0.30, 0.1×ATR14)); TP exactly 1:1. Lots = balance×0.01 ÷ (stopDist × 100 oz), rounded to 0.01.

Fills — simulated on candle high/low touch; if SL and TP are touched in the same candle it is counted a loss (conservative).

Paper trading only — no real orders are placed. Simulated fills use candle OHLC, not tick data. Paste ?selftest=1 in the URL to run the built-in logic test.
